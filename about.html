Our Story

Powering Homes & Businesses Across Chhattisgarh

Established in Bilaspur, Rudra Electricals has been at the forefront of the solar energy revolution in Chhattisgarh. Founded with a mission to make clean, affordable energy accessible to every household and business, we have grown from a local electrical services provider to a full-spectrum solar energy solutions company.

Today, we serve customers across Bilaspur, Champa, Mungeli, Korba and surrounding areas — with over 100 successful rooftop solar installations and counting. We are proud to be MNRE empanelled and are authorized installers for leading brands including Adani Solar, Waaree, Tata Power SolarRoof and Havells.

Our tagline says it all — सूरज की ऊर्जा, हर घर की रोशनी — we believe solar energy should light up every home in India.
